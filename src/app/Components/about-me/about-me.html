About Me

Welcome to my portfolio! I’m Aya, a front-end developer who loves turning ideas into clean, interactive interfaces using Angular. With hands-on experience building responsive web applications, I focus on writing scalable TypeScript, modular components, and maintainable styles that look great on any screen.

My strengths include building reusable UI components, crafting smooth animations with CSS, and implementing pixel-perfect designs from Figma or custom concepts. I enjoy working with modern Angular features, integrating APIs, and optimizing performance so every project feels fast, intuitive, and reliable.

I’m passionate about design details—colors, typography, and micro-interactions—and I often blend my interest in calligraphy and branding into my work to create unique visual identities. Every project is an opportunity to learn something new, improve the user experience, and deliver interfaces that feel both professional and personal.
